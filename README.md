## How I approach a Business Analyst assignment

1. **Clarify the decision:** What does the business need to decide or improve?
2. **Define the scope:** Which customer journey, process, team, location and time period are involved?
3. **Map the current state:** Identify activities, owners, systems, handoffs, exceptions and dependencies.
4. **Establish the evidence:** Validate data definitions, completeness, time periods and stakeholder input.
5. **Find the cause:** Separate symptoms from root causes; test competing explanations.
6. **Specify the change:** Document requirements, options, priorities, risks and acceptance criteria.
7. **Measure the result:** Agree baselines, KPI owners, review cadence and post-change checks.

Typical deliverables include a problem statement, process map, requirements log, stakeholder matrix, root-cause analysis, KPI dictionary, recommendation paper and implementation action tracker.
