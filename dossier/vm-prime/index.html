01 / IDENTITY

One company. Two complementary layers.

VM-PRIME is the branded portfolio entry. ETHIC is the intended Behavior Engineering company; MANRA is the strategic intelligence and discovery system behind its product decisions.

ETHIC · THE PRODUCT THESIS

Behavior Engineering

Build affordable, useful physical products around persistent friction in everyday behaviour. The long-term category is broader than any one gadget or product family.

MANRA · THE DECISION SYSTEM

Strategic product discovery

Investigate real-world patterns, unofficial workarounds, existing products and residual failures. Reject attractive ideas when evidence, feasibility or differentiation does not survive scrutiny.

02 / METHOD

From an observed behaviour to a justified build.

The discovery sequence is designed to stop weak ideas early and keep assumptions separate from verified facts.

01Observe friction

Find a recurring situation where people adjust, improvise, repeat or tolerate a failure.

02Capture workarounds

Document the unofficial “second interface” people create to make existing things cooperate.

03Test the residual gap

Check whether existing commercial products already absorb the workaround and whether a specific unmet need remains.

04Prototype the minimum

Build the simplest physical intervention that can prove or disprove the proposed mechanism.

05Qualify, then scale

Verify usability, costs, repeatability, product landscape, manufacturing path and evidence before promoting a candidate.

Hypothesis · Not cleared
F2 · FinishingPrecedent

Consistent edge finishing

F3 · StorageCase study

Reducing the footprint of tools

Fold-flat measuring spoons show how a small physical change can reorganise storage. It is an external product precedent, not an ETHIC product.

F4 · Retention / cleaning context

When a cord or interface makes a tool awkward to use

Inspect the actual use environment and the recurring failure mode before proposing a product. A portable form alone does not prove that a tool works in every location or for every cleaning task.

Rule: separate observed behaviour, source-supported facts and hypotheses. Keep C1 as a candidate until the physical tests and qualification gates are passed.
