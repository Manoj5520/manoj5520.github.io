CASE 02 · MARKET INTELLIGENCEApplied experience

Competitor benchmarking linked to service-line opportunity

NexaHomes · Hyderabad residential real-estate landscape

Business question
Where could the business differentiate in a competitive local market while remaining consistent with its positioning and delivery model?
Method
Compared competitor positioning and service offers—including players such as My Home Group and ASBL—alongside customer requirements and observed service gaps.
Decision output
Service-line opportunities were assessed against customer fit, differentiation and NexaHomes’ positioning rather than treating every market gap as an automatic opportunity.
Transferable value
Evidence-led market scanning, structured comparison, opportunity screening and translating research into a practical business choice.

Impact boundary: This is applied competitor-research experience. The page does not publish confidential competitor notes or claim quantified revenue attributable to the analysis. The downloadable benchmark is a method illustration, not a record of every historical finding.

NexaHomes · Excel and Power BI reporting

Business need
