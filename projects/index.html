01 / Business Operations Improvement

Download one-page PDF ↓

Context: Sales-to-delivery handoff friction in a residential real-estate operating context.

  1. Process: Customer requirement → confirmed scope → handoff checklist → named delivery owner → vendor/internal execution → customer update → closure.
  2. Problem hypothesis: Handoff details split across messages and records can cause unclear ownership, repeated follow-ups and missed actions.
  3. Proposed controls: Standard handoff record, named owner, due date, exception log and consistent customer-update cadence.
  4. Metrics to track: Handoff completeness, time to owner acceptance, overdue actions, rework frequency and on-time closure.

02 / Market & Competitive Intelligence

Download one-page PDF ↓

Context: A comparison framework for residential brands such as My Home and ASBL; not a claim that every competitor fact has been independently verified here.

  • Compare location, customer segment, product positioning, enquiry journey, post-sale support, and publicly stated differentiators.
  • Record source, date checked, observable evidence and confidence for every finding.
  • Separate verified observations from hypotheses; missing public information is not proof a service does not exist.
  • Output: executive summary, evidence-backed differentiators, research gaps and opportunities to validate.

03 / Operational Reporting

Download one-page PDF ↓

Example source fields: Case ID, date opened, channel, stage, owner, due date, status, blocker, date closed.

  • Excel pivot views: open cases by stage/owner, overdue actions by age band, opened vs. closed weekly, blocker frequency and closure time.
  • Quality checks: unique IDs, consistent status values, valid dates, visible reporting period and documented refresh date.
  • Management use: highlight exceptions, assign owners, agree actions and review trends. This demonstrates basic reporting design—not advanced Power BI development.
